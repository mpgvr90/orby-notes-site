English

ORBY Notes is an offline notes app. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • No account, no server. The app has no sign-up and does not send your notes anywhere. Core features work without internet.
  • Your notes stay on your device. Notes, tags, categories, attachments and settings are stored locally in the app's private storage. The database is not encrypted; the optional PIN and biometric lock protect access to the app's interface.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Photos and camera. Used only when you choose to attach an image. The image is copied into the app's local storage and never uploaded.
  • Face ID / fingerprint. Handled entirely by your operating system. The app only receives a yes/no result and never sees biometric data.
  • Backups and sharing. Exports, backups and shared notes are created only when you ask and go only to the place you choose. On Android, app data is excluded from automatic cloud backup. On iOS, app data may be part of your own device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have device backups turned on.
  • Links. Links you tap in a note open in your browser; the app itself sends nothing.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
  • Deleting data. Delete notes in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from the device.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
