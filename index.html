FAQ

Where are my notes stored? Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync.

How do I move notes to a new phone? Settings → Backup & Privacy → Create full backup (.zip). Move the file to the new phone and use Restore from backup there.

I forgot my PIN. The PIN is stored only on your device and cannot be recovered by us. Make regular backups.

Does the app need internet? No. Links inside notes open in your browser.
